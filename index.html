The Narrative

An Eternal Conflict of Cosmic Balance

"When Dharma is threatened by the shadows of Lanka, the path of righteousness must be forged across empires, wilderness, and oceans."

I

The Royal Exile

Begin in the golden towers of Ayodhya. Strategize your resources, assemble the royal weaponry, and travel southwards into exile under the guidance of ancient sages.

II

The Dark Forest Trials

Navigate the treacherous, demon-infested woods of Dandakaranya. Outsmart Ravan's shadow agents and protect the sages to gain celestial blessings and mystic weapons.

III

The Ocean Siege

Forge alliances with the Vanara kingdom of Kishkindha. Construct the legendary Ram Setu bridge across the roaring southern seas, and lay siege to Ravan's golden fort.
